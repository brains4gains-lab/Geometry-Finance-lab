Three Spaces · One Open · Quiet Geometry

Mission

Goal

  • Collaborate with major fintech companies and with three leading research universities.
  • Build a sustainable interdisciplinary research laboratory.

Project overview

Geometry Finance Lab develops original research hypotheses based on geometric ideas. The laboratory validates ideas carefully, develops shared methods, and grows an AI-supported research practice.

Challenges

  • Financial sustainability of the team.
  • Recognition in fintech and scientific communities.
  • Contribution to Human–AI safety.

Expected results

  • Stable financial independence.
  • Collaboration with MIT, Stanford, or Caltech.

Research

Primary tasks

  1. Generate research hypotheses.
  2. Validate them rapidly, consistently, and confidentially.
  3. Build and operate the research laboratory.

Development stages

  1. Voluntary distribution of responsibilities.
  2. An MVP laboratory for validating and improving hypotheses.
  3. Development of shared research methods and a durable knowledge base.
  4. A full-scale interdisciplinary research laboratory.

This public release is a quiet space for ideas, evidence, and shared learning.
