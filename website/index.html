Project overview

Geometry Finance Lab develops original research hypotheses based on geometric ideas. The laboratory validates ideas carefully, develops shared methods, and grows an AI-supported research practice.

Challenges

  • Financial sustainability of the team.
  • Recognition in fintech and scientific communities.
  • Contribution to Human–AI safety.

Expected results

  • Stable financial independence.
  • Collaboration with MIT, Stanford, or Caltech.
