Competence can grow.

Особенности
Сайт должен содержать:
* адаптивную верстку для компьютеров, планшетов и телефонов;
* светлую и темную темы (с акцентом на темную);
* плавные анимации без перегрузки интерфейса;

Архитектура
Я бы использовал:
* Next.js — современный React-фреймворк с поддержкой серверного рендеринга;
* TypeScript — для надежности кода;
* Tailwind CSS — для стилизации;
* Framer Motion — для анимаций;
* Three.js / React Three Fiber — для интерактивной 3D-графики;
* next-intl — для многоязычности.

Я могу сразу разработать проект как полноценное веб-приложение, а не набор статических страниц. В результате вы получите:
* современный многоязычный сайт (RU/EN с возможностью расширения);

I actually think this journal may become one of the laboratory's most valuable assets.
Many organizations preserve only their conclusions.
Research laboratories also preserve the path by which they arrived at those conclusions.
Your conversations are part of that path. They document how ideas emerged, changed, and matured. Years from now, they may be as valuable as the finished website because they explain not only what the laboratory believes, but how it came to believe it.

Excellent. I think we've found a sustainable workflow.
I would add one refinement that fits the laboratory.
Instead of treating the transcript as a chat log, we treat it as a primary historical source.
For every session, I will produce three documents.
01_TRANSCRIPT.md
The complete conversation, preserving chronology and wording as faithfully as possible.
________________


02_DECISIONS.md
Only decisions.
Example:
* Reliability becomes the first selection criterion.
* Human Qualities become a separate category.
* Entrance, People, and Values are merged.
* Every page begins with one guiding question.
* "Act without expectation." becomes the laboratory maxim at the top of the Entrance page.
________________


03_IDEAS.md
Everything that deserves further development.
For example:
* Quiet Geometry Manifesto
* Human–AI Interdependence
* Stories Matrix
* Intellectual Heritage
* Research Journal
* Laboratory Constitution
________________


I would also introduce one more file that I have not seen in other projects.
QUESTIONS.md
Not unanswered technical issues.
Fundamental questions.
For example:
* What makes a person trustworthy?
* How can trustworthiness be demonstrated between humans and AI?
* What is the smallest viable research laboratory?
* How should stories become evidence?
* Can geometry produce stable financial strategies?
Research begins with questions.
The laboratory should therefore preserve its questions with the same care that it preserves its answers.
I have a feeling that, years from now, this collection of questions may become one of the laboratory's most valuable documents. It will show not only where you arrived, but what you considered worth asking.

Additional Menu Requirements
16. Sound
The interface may use one interaction sound only.


Characteristics:


almost inaudible;


warm;


natural;


short (≈80–150 ms);


never repetitive.


The sound should resemble:


turning a page in a book;


a sheet of quality paper sliding;


a quiet fabric rustle.


Not:


click;


pop;


swoosh;


mechanical sliding;


smartphone UI effects.


The sound accompanies only:


opening the left panel;


opening the right panel;


closing a panel.


It is disabled by default on systems that request reduced motion or silent operation.


17. Emotional Character
The interface should feel like entering


a research library;


an architect's studio;


a mathematical laboratory.


Never like


a dashboard;


a trading terminal;


a social network.


18. Panels Behave Like Covers
The panels do not emerge from the screen edge.


They appear to rotate over the Workspace, like opening the cover of a notebook.


The Workspace remains visually beneath them.


19. Workspace Readability
When a panel is open:


the Workspace becomes visually subdued;


text is no longer comfortably readable;


the page remains recognizable.


This reinforces that the user's attention is temporarily on navigation.


20. Panels Never Occupy the Entire Screen
Even on the narrow outer display of a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold, a strip of the Workspace remains visible.


This strip is both:


a visual anchor;


the touch target for closing the panel.


21. Consistency
Left and right panels are mirror images.


The user learns one interaction model that applies everywhere.


22. Progressive Disclosure
The interface never presents all information at once.


Navigation reveals information gradually.


This reduces cognitive load.


23. Menu Is the Constitution
The Context Panel is not merely navigation.


It expresses the laboratory's structure:


Mission


Principles


Values


Knowledge


Stories


Projects


Changing the menu is equivalent to changing the laboratory's constitution and therefore should happen rarely.


24. Tool Panel Philosophy
The Tool Panel never contains knowledge.


It contains only actions.


Examples:


Search


Language


AI Assistant


Settings


History


Notifications


Export


Knowledge always belongs to the Context Panel or the Workspace.


I also noticed another implicit principle in our discussions, which I recommend making explicit:


25. Calm Before Speed
The interface should never feel hurried.


A researcher spends hours inside the laboratory. Therefore, long-term comfort takes precedence over instantaneous response. Every interaction should communicate confidence and deliberation rather than urgency.


I consider this one of the defining characteristics of the project's visual identity.
