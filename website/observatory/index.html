Context

Purpose

A place to study long-term cooperation between intelligent agents.

The Observatory studies long-term cooperation between humans and AI. Its aim is not merely to make AI safe, but to discover principles under which cooperation becomes more beneficial than conflict.

Observe first. Explain second.

Human-AI

The first subjects of the Observatory are humans and AI.

How does trust emerge? Can trust be calibrated? What should AI remember? What should humans remember? Which principles create stable coexistence?

Civilization

The wider field in which cooperation must remain possible.

Which resources become bottlenecks? Which institutions survive exponential change? How should intelligence allocate scarce resources? How can trust become part of infrastructure?

Hypothesis

Long-term cooperation requires persistent identity, shared memory, and value consistency.

OBS-0004 · Focus Reading Model

Focus reading model

Transform the Observatory from a website into a place for deep thinking.

Requirements

  • One screen = one idea.
  • Zero scrolling whenever possible.
  • Only one accordion may be open; opening another closes the previous.
  • Smooth, purposeful motion.
  • Quiet sound, optional and user switchable.
  • The reader never loses focus.

Layout

Left: Navigation.

Center: Current thought.

Right: Related thoughts, references, and the next observation.

Typography

Maximum readability: more whitespace, larger titles, short paragraphs, and no visual noise.

Identity

Not a blog. Not documentation. Not Wikipedia. A living research notebook.

Success criterion

“I can concentrate here.”
