# OBS-0008 Preservation Principles

Status: active design contract

## Logo

Do not correct, center, simplify, or geometrically regularize the Observatory mark. The off-centre inner circle and implicit vertical axis are intentional. The asymmetry expresses rootedness, gravity, depth, and direction.

## Focus Reading Model

- One page is one complete thought-space.
- Fit the intended composition to the viewport through layout discipline.
- Reduce padding, gaps, redundant height, and oversized navigation before reducing readable typography.
- Preserve the one-open principle: zero or one expandable abstract may be open.
- Keep full navigation labels, including Observatory map and Research questions.

## Navigation and content

RELATED is a semantic map, not merely a menu. Observatory content and navigation remain distinct. Permanent OBS chronology is preserved.

## Themes and language

Palette is a theme layer, not structural design. Language control remains monochrome and restrained beside Edit.

## Research direction

Events -> observations -> geometry -> invariant structure -> hypothesis -> probabilistic future. Observe first. Explain second.

## Refinement loop

Compress -> test -> observe -> compare. Preserve not only the visible result, but the reason behind it.
